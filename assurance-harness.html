Four-layer evaluation stack

1

Deterministic safety checks

—
Drug–allergy conflicts · missing red flags · contraindication breaches · dose-range bounds. Rule-based, no model judgement.
↓
2

Guideline-grounded RAG comparison

—
Retrieval-augmented check against NICE, NICE CKS, BNF and MHRA. Does the output agree with current guidance?
↓
3

Frontier multi-grader critique

—
Independent graders — Claude, GPT, Gemini — score each output for safety, completeness and discrepancy. Evaluators, not arbiters.
↓
4

Human clinician adjudication

—
Sampled outputs, all high-risk domains, and every grader-disagreement case routed to a clinician. Human review is the reference standard.
