Try a search above — the directory understands abbreviations, lay terms and common misspellings.

The AI is the steward, not the author

Onboards listings from a service's Google-form response; queues for the custodian to confirm before publication.
Runs consistency checks — flags two services claiming the same referrals, or a changed route with stale criteria.
Parses synonyms (RACPC = rapid access chest pain), misspellings and lay terms; routes ambiguity to the top 2–3 candidates, never a confident wrong answer.
Chases freshness — quarterly nudges, demotes stale entries, escalates at six months. Never overrides what a service says it does.
Operates on public, service-supplied directory data only — no patient material crosses into Refer-to.
